The Program

What Is The Alberta Advantage Immigration Program?

The AAIP is an economic immigration program run by the governments of Alberta and Canada. It nominates people who can fill Alberta's job shortages, and people who will buy or start businesses in the province. If you are nominated, you apply to the Government of Canada for permanent residence together with your spouse or common law partner and dependent children.

You cannot simply apply to most streams. Worker streams select candidates from a shared Worker Expression of Interest pool, scored out of 100. Entrepreneur streams run their own Expression of Interest processes with their own points grids. Either way, the sequence is the same: prove you fit a stream, enter its pool, get invited, then apply.

How It Works

How AAIP Selection Works, In Plain Order

1

Choose The Right Stream

Eligibility differs completely by stream. The same person can be a strong candidate in one stream and ineligible in another, so the stream decision comes before any forms.

2

Enter The Pool

Workers submit one Worker Expression of Interest for $135 and are scored out of 100. Entrepreneurs submit the EOI their stream requires, with its own grid and documents.

3

Get Invited, Then Apply

Alberta invites candidates based on score rankings, labour market needs and available nominations. Invited candidates complete the full application and pay the stream's fee.

One Program, Eight Doors

People still search for the Alberta PNP or the AINP. It is the same program: Alberta renamed it the Alberta Advantage Immigration Program in 2022. The name changed; the logic did not. Alberta nominates for its own economic needs, and every stream is a different answer to a different need.

The practical consequence: there is no general AAIP application. There are eight specific ones, and the first professional job is picking the door your facts actually fit.

The Reality

Where AAIP Candidates Go Wrong

Entering The Wrong Stream

A worker on a PGWP from another province, an entrepreneur without community support, a founder without an agency: each is a good candidate somewhere and a declined application where they actually filed.

Confusing The Scores

The Worker EOI score out of 100, the federal CRS score, and the entrepreneur points grids are three different numbers for three different systems. Optimizing the wrong one wastes the permit time you cannot get back.

Assuming The Rules Stand Still

This program changes in public: the EOI system replaced direct applications in September 2024, the family connection pathway ended in March 2025, and Rural Renewal tightened its endorsement rules for January 2026. Advice from last year can be wrong this year.

Treating Fees As The Risk

Government fees are non refundable, but they are the small loss. The real cost of a wrong filing is months of permit validity and a place in a pool you were never competitive in.

Why Files Fail

Why AAIP Applications Fail Across All Streams

Eligibility That Expires Before Assessment

Streams check eligibility when you apply and again when Alberta assesses you. Permits, language tests, endorsements and job offers all carry expiry dates, and the file has to survive both checks.

Documents That Do Not Prove The Points

Every point claimed in an EOI has to be provable at application. Scores built on optimistic readings of experience, ownership or funds collapse at verification.

A Missing Third Party

Employers must complete declarations. Communities must issue endorsements and support letters. Agencies must recommend founders. Files that treat those parties as paperwork, instead of people to brief early, stall waiting for them.

The Wrong Occupation Story

Your occupation code runs through your EOI, your job offer and your experience record. When those three tell different stories, assessors decline rather than reconcile them.

Questions

Alberta PNP (AAIP) Questions, Answered

Is the Alberta PNP the same as the AAIP?

Yes. The program was renamed the Alberta Advantage Immigration Program in 2022. Alberta PNP and AINP are the older names, and most candidates still search for them. The streams, fees and selection systems described here are the current program.

Can I apply to AAIP directly?

For the worker streams, no. You submit a Worker Expression of Interest and Alberta invites selected candidates to apply. The entrepreneur streams also begin with an Expression of Interest, except the Farm Stream, which takes a direct application with a proposed business plan.

Do I need a job offer?

It depends on the stream. The Opportunity, Rural Renewal and Tourism and Hospitality streams are built on Alberta job offers. The Express Entry pathways for tech, health care and law enforcement also require Alberta offers, while priority sector selections can be based on occupation alone. The entrepreneur streams require a business, not a job offer.

How much does AAIP cost?

The Worker Expression of Interest fee is $135, and the worker stream application fee is $1,500 once you are invited. Entrepreneur streams have their own published fee schedule on alberta.ca. All government fees are non refundable. Our professional fees are confirmed with you in writing before work starts.

I am not sure which stream fits me. What happens if I ask?

That is the normal starting point. Tell us your status, occupation, education and plans in the form below, and we will assess you against all eight streams and tell you which one your facts fit, or what to change so that one does.

Can you promise a nomination?

No, and no consultant honestly can. Alberta decides nominations and the Government of Canada decides permanent residence. What we do is prepare your case so the stream choice is right, the eligibility is real at both checks, and every point you claim is documented.
