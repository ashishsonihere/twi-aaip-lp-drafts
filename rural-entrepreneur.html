The Stream

What Is the Rural Entrepreneur Stream?

The Rural Entrepreneur Stream is an economic immigration program for entrepreneurs who want to start a new business or buy an existing business in a rural Alberta community. Alberta defines rural as a community with a population under 100,000 that sits outside the Calgary and Edmonton Census Metropolitan Areas.

You submit an Expression of Interest with your Business Proposal Summary, your Net Worth Worksheet and your Community Support Letter. Alberta scores it on the stream's points grid, covering your location, experience, investment, net worth, job creation, language, education and adaptability. The highest ranking candidates are requested to submit a Business Application.

Eligibility

Who This Stream Fits, And Who It Does Not

You Likely Qualify If

  • You have at least 3 years of experience as an active business owner or manager, or 4 years as a senior manager, within the past 10 years
  • Your net worth is at least $300,000 and you can invest at least $100,000 of your own equity into the business
  • You will own at least 51% of a new business, or 100% of an existing business you buy out completely
  • Your business will create at least one full time job for a Canadian citizen or permanent resident who is not a relative
  • You have completed the exploratory visit and hold a Community Support Letter from a participating rural community
  • You meet CLB 4 in each language skill and hold at least a high school education with an Educational Credential Assessment

This Stream Will Not Work If

  • Your chosen community has a population of 100,000 or more, or sits inside the Calgary or Edmonton Census Metropolitan Area
  • You skip the exploratory visit or the Community Support Letter. Both are mandatory, and the EOI cannot be submitted without the letter
  • Your business partners are not Canadian citizens or permanent residents
  • Your investment falls below $100,000 or your net worth below $300,000
  • Your proposed business is on Alberta's ineligible businesses list
  • You plan to be an absentee owner. This stream is scored and assessed on you living in the community and running the business

The Reality

The Real Challenges Rural Entrepreneur Candidates Face

Choosing The Town Is A Points Decision

Location is the single largest factor on the points grid. A community under 10,000 people scores the maximum rural points; a town near 100,000 scores the fewest. The right town is a business decision and a scoring decision at the same time.

The Community Has To Choose You

Communities meet only with entrepreneurs they judge to be a fit, and they issue support letters for proposals they believe benefit the town. A generic proposal that could be anywhere reads as a risk to the people deciding.

Net Worth Has To Be Provable

Your $300,000 must be documented as your own or your spouse's holdings, and your $100,000 investment must come from your own equity. Money that cannot be traced cannot be scored.

Succession Is A Different File

Buying an existing business means 100% ownership and a complete change of control, and job creation is not required. It also means valuing a rural business, its staff and its books, in a market you may not know yet.

Why Files Fail

Why Rural Entrepreneur Files Stall Or Get Declined

No Community Support Letter

Without the letter and the endorsed Business Proposal Summary, the EOI cannot proceed. Candidates who build the business plan first and meet the community last have the order backwards.

The Community Is Not Rural Under The Rules

A town inside the Calgary or Edmonton metro area, or at or above 100,000 people, is not eligible no matter how rural it feels. The census decides, not the impression.

Ownership Below The Minimum

Less than 51% of a new business, or anything short of a complete buyout in a succession, fails the establishment criteria.

The Exploratory Visit Report Is Thin

The visit report must document who you met, what you discussed and how it shaped the proposal, including business cards and service providers. A visit that leaves no paper trail reads as a visit that did not happen.

The Business Is On The Ineligible List

Certain business types cannot be nominated under this stream at all. Checking the list before you build the proposal costs nothing and saves the entire project.

How It Works

How The Rural Entrepreneur Process Runs

1

Explore And Visit

Research participating communities, contact the community's contact person, and complete your exploratory visit in person or by video conference. Keep records of every meeting for your visit report.

2

Proposal And Community Support

Give the community your Business Proposal Summary. If the community supports the proposal, it issues your Community Support Letter and endorses the summary.

3

EOI And Business Application

Submit your Expression of Interest with the support letter and net worth worksheet. If Alberta ranks you highly enough, you are requested to submit the full Business Application, including the Exploratory Visit Report.

The Community Decides With You

Most immigration streams are a conversation between you and a government. This one starts with a town. The community's support letter tells Alberta that local people, who will live with the result, have examined your proposal and want it to happen.

That is why the visit and the proposal matter more here than raw money. A $100,000 investment with a community behind it outranks a larger investment that no town has endorsed.

Requirements

What You Will Need

Your Side

  • Proof of 3 years as a business owner or manager, or 4 years as a senior manager, within the last 10 years
  • Documented net worth of at least $300,000 and at least $100,000 of investable equity
  • Language results at CLB 4 or better, under 2 years old, and an ECA for your education
  • An Exploratory Visit Report documenting your community research and meetings

The Business Side

  • A Business Proposal Summary the community is willing to endorse
  • A Community Support Letter from a participating rural community
  • A business that is not on Alberta's ineligible businesses list
  • A job creation plan for at least one full time position, unless you are buying out an existing business completely
Questions

Rural Entrepreneur Stream Questions, Answered

Does the exploratory visit have to be in person?

Not necessarily. You and the community decide together whether the visit is in person or completed by web based video conferencing. Either way, an Exploratory Visit Report must be submitted with your Business Application. Note that neither AAIP nor the community will provide a letter of support to help you obtain a visa for an in person visit.

Can I buy an existing business instead of starting one?

Yes. That is called business succession. You must acquire 100% ownership so the buyout results in a complete change of control, and unlike a new business, job creation is not required, although creating jobs still scores points.

How much money do I actually need?

The minimums are a net worth of $300,000 and an investment of $100,000 from your own equity. Higher investment amounts score more points on the grid, so the minimum that qualifies and the amount that competes are two different numbers. We will show you both before you commit.

What is the difference between this stream and the Farm Stream?

This stream is for businesses in rural communities: retail, services, trades, manufacturing and similar ventures. The Farm Stream is specifically for primary production farming, requires a minimum $500,000 equity investment and a $500,000 net worth, and its business plans are reviewed by Alberta Agriculture and Irrigation. If your plan is a farm, we assess you under the Farm Stream rules.

Can my business partner be a foreign national?

No. Business partners under this stream must be Canadian citizens or permanent residents.

Can you promise my EOI will be selected?

No, and no consultant honestly can. Alberta selects the highest ranking expressions of interest and decides every nomination. What we do is prepare your proposal, your visit and your file so the community can support you with confidence and your score reflects everything the grid rewards.
