The Stream

What Is the Graduate Entrepreneur Stream?

The Graduate Entrepreneur Stream allows Alberta to nominate qualified international graduates from Alberta post secondary institutions who want to establish or operate a business in Alberta. You submit an Expression of Interest with a Business Proposal Summary and a Net Worth Worksheet, Alberta scores it, and the highest ranking candidates are invited to submit a Business Application.

One point of confusion deserves a straight answer. Alberta's published criteria for this stream do not set a deadline by which you must have graduated. The two year figure in the rules is the minimum length of your Alberta study, not a countdown after graduation. Your real clock is your Post Graduation Work Permit: it must be valid on the day your EOI goes in.

Eligibility

Who This Stream Fits, And Who It Does Not

You Likely Qualify If

  • You completed at least two years of full time education, resulting in a degree or diploma from an Alberta Advanced Education publicly funded post secondary institution
  • You hold a Post Graduation Work Permit that is valid at the time you submit your Expression of Interest
  • You meet CLB 7 in each language skill, with test results less than 2 years old
  • You will establish a new business or buy an existing business in Alberta with at least 34% ownership
  • Your business partners, if any, are Canadian citizens or permanent residents
  • Your proposed business is not on Alberta's ineligible businesses list

This Stream Will Not Work If

  • Your degree or diploma is from outside Alberta. Graduates of institutions outside Canada belong in the Foreign Graduate Entrepreneur Stream instead
  • Your PGWP has expired or will expire before your EOI is submitted
  • Your Alberta program was shorter than two years of full time study
  • Your language score is below CLB 7 in any one of the four skills
  • You would own less than 34% of the business
  • You already hold an active AAIP nomination under another stream. Candidates are eligible for only one nomination certificate

The Reality

The Real Challenges Alberta Graduates Face

The PGWP Clock

Your permit must be valid when the EOI is submitted, and PGWPs are not renewable. Graduates who spend their permit years in survival jobs and start thinking about this stream in the final months often run out of runway.

Points Are Won In Adaptability

The grid rewards more than the business idea: business experience or entrepreneurship training, age 21 to 49, a spouse working or studying in Alberta, and Alberta relatives all score. Graduates who ignore the adaptability factors compete with one hand tied.

The Business Comes After Approval

You are not required to establish the business until your Business Application is approved. That sequencing matters for leases, loans and purchases, and getting it backwards can strand your money in a business Alberta has not accepted.

Ownership Structure Mistakes

Dropping below 34% ownership, or taking on a partner who is not a Canadian citizen or permanent resident, fails the establishment criteria no matter how good the business is.

Why Files Fail

Why Graduate Entrepreneur Files Stall Or Get Declined

The PGWP Was Not Valid At EOI

The permit check happens at submission. An EOI filed on an expired permit does not meet the criteria, and there is no discretion to overlook it.

The Credential Does Not Qualify

The education must be at least two years of full time study at an Alberta publicly funded post secondary institution, resulting in a degree or diploma. Shorter programs and non qualifying institutions do not meet the requirement.

Language Below CLB 7

This stream's language bar is higher than the other entrepreneur streams. A CLB 6 in even one skill means the mandatory minimum is not met.

The Business Is On The Ineligible List

Certain business types are excluded from this stream. The list has to be checked against your actual business activity, not your industry label.

The Score Is Not Competitive

Meeting the minimums only enters you in the pool. Alberta requests Business Applications from the highest ranking EOIs, so a file that qualifies but scores low can wait indefinitely.

How It Works

How The Graduate Entrepreneur Process Runs

1

Submit Your EOI

With a valid PGWP, you submit your Expression of Interest with your Business Proposal Summary and Net Worth Worksheet. Alberta scores it on the 125 point grid.

2

Business Application

If Alberta ranks you highly enough, you are requested to submit a Business Application. Your EOI points and eligibility are verified at this stage.

3

Establish And Operate

Once the Business Application is approved, you establish or purchase the business and operate it in Alberta, then proceed through nomination and the federal permanent residence stage.

Built For Alberta Graduates

Alberta educated you, and this stream is how the province keeps the benefit of that education. The grid is designed to reward graduates who are already rooted here: Alberta study is mandatory, Alberta business experience and Alberta family score points, and rural business locations score more.

If you are still studying, the most valuable thing you can do is plan backwards from your PGWP expiry date. The stream rewards preparation measured in years, not weeks.

Questions

Graduate Entrepreneur Stream Questions, Answered

Do I have to have graduated within the last two years?

No. That rule does not appear in Alberta's published criteria for this stream, and we correct it wherever we see it repeated. The two year requirement is the minimum length of your study: at least two years of full time education in Alberta. The deadline that actually governs you is your Post Graduation Work Permit, which must be valid when your EOI is submitted.

Can I use a degree from another province or another country?

Not in this stream. This stream requires an Alberta credential. If you graduated outside Canada, look at the Foreign Graduate Entrepreneur Stream, which is built for foreign degrees and works through designated agencies.

Do I need to open the business before I apply?

No. Candidates are not required to establish the business until their Business Application is approved by AAIP. There is also a route for graduates who already own and operate an Alberta business that has been operating for at least one year before the EOI.

What score do I need?

There is no published pass mark. Alberta scores EOIs on a 125 point grid and requests Business Applications from the highest ranking candidates. We will score your profile against the grid honestly, including the adaptability factors students most often miss, before you file.

Can you promise my EOI will be selected?

No, and no consultant honestly can. Alberta decides who is requested to apply and who is nominated. What we do is prepare your EOI, proposal and documents so your score reflects everything you are entitled to claim and your business case reads the way an assessor needs it to.
