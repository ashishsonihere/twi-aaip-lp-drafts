The Stream

What Is the Alberta Opportunity Stream?

The Alberta Opportunity Stream is for temporary foreign workers who are already living and working full time in Alberta and who have a full time job offer from an Alberta employer in an eligible occupation. You cannot apply directly. You submit a Worker Expression of Interest, Alberta scores it out of 100, and if you are selected you are invited to apply.

Eligibility is checked twice: when you submit your application and again when Alberta assesses it. A permit, a job or a language score that was valid when you started can quietly stop being valid while you wait. That timing trap is where most Alberta workers lose this stream, and it is avoidable.

Eligibility

Who This Stream Fits, And Who It Does Not

These are the stream's actual rules, published by the Alberta Advantage Immigration Program. Read both columns before you spend money on an Expression of Interest.

You Likely Qualify If

  • You hold a valid work permit based on a positive LMIA, or an eligible LMIA exemption such as International Experience Canada, an intra company transfer, a trade agreement or Mobilité Francophone
  • Or you hold a Post Graduation Work Permit from an Alberta publicly funded post secondary institution, and your job is related to your Alberta field of study
  • You work full time in Alberta in an eligible occupation, and your employer will give you a full time job offer or contract in that same occupation
  • You meet the language floor for your occupation: CLB 5 for TEER 0 to 3, CLB 4 for TEER 4 to 5, and CLB 7 if your occupation is NOC 33102
  • You have at least a high school education equivalent to Canadian standards, proven with an Educational Credential Assessment
  • You have 12 months of full time Alberta experience in your current occupation in the last 18 months, or 24 months in Canada or abroad in the last 30 months

This Stream Will Not Work If

  • You are on maintained status (formerly called implied status) or restoration status. Alberta does not accept either for this stream
  • Your Post Graduation Work Permit comes from a college or university outside Alberta. For this stream, a PGWP only counts from an Alberta publicly funded institution
  • You live or work in another province, or you do not hold valid temporary resident status
  • Your job is part time, casual or seasonal, or you work as an independent contractor or through a temporary agency
  • You work from home in a business that is not zoned for commercial or industrial operations, or you work virtually from outside Alberta
  • Your occupation is on Alberta's ineligible occupations list, which includes teachers, actors, casino workers and harvesting labourers, among others

The Reality

The Real Challenges Alberta Workers Face

Based on the files we see from Alberta workers, these are the problems that actually stall or sink Opportunity Stream applications.

Your Permit Clock Runs While You Wait

Selection is not instant. Your work permit has to be valid when you apply and still valid when Alberta assesses you, and maintained or restoration status does not count. Workers who file and then let a permit lapse lose the stream entirely.

Your Employer Is Part Of Your Application

Your employer has to meet Alberta's job offer and employer requirements and complete the Employer Declaration and Authorization form. Many good applications die because nobody briefed the employer early enough.

Your Experience Must Match Your Current Occupation

Experience is counted in your current occupation only. Years in a different job, however skilled, do not build your 12 or 24 months, and part time hours do not count toward them.

Two Different Scores Get Confused

Your Worker EOI score out of 100 is Alberta's own grid. It is not your federal Express Entry CRS score, and a strong CRS does not raise your Alberta ranking. Workers waste months optimizing the wrong number.

Why Files Fail

Why Opportunity Stream Applications Stall Or Get Declined

The Permit Was Not Valid At Assessment

Eligibility is rechecked when Alberta assesses the file. A permit that expired, or a status that slipped into maintained or restoration, ends the application even if everything else was in order.

The Job Offer Did Not Meet The Rules

Part time, casual, seasonal, agency and remote arrangements are ineligible, and the job must be on premises in Alberta at a commercially zoned workplace. Offers that look full time on paper fail on these details.

The Language Test Was Too Low Or Too Old

Scores below the floor for your TEER category, or results older than 2 years at submission, mean the application does not meet the criteria. NOC 33102 workers are caught out by the higher CLB 7 requirement.

The Education Assessment Was Missing

Foreign education needs an Educational Credential Assessment showing at least Canadian high school equivalence. Files submitted without one, or with an ECA that does not recognize the institution, do not meet the education requirement.

The Occupation Was Ineligible

A published list of occupations cannot apply under this stream at all. Checking your NOC code against that list takes minutes and saves the entire fee.

Your EOI Score Is A Ranking, Not A Pass Mark

There is no fixed passing score for the Alberta Opportunity Stream. Alberta invites candidates based on EOI score rankings, provincial labour market needs and priorities, and the number of nominations available. A score that is not invited this month can be invited later, and a strong score in an occupation Alberta does not need right now can wait.

What you control is the accuracy and strength of what you declare: the right occupation, the right experience counted the right way, and documents that prove every point you claim. That is where preparation changes outcomes.

Requirements

What You Will Need

Your Side

  • A valid work permit of an eligible type, valid at submission and at assessment
  • Language test results under 2 years old, at or above the floor for your TEER category
  • An Educational Credential Assessment for your highest education, unless you hold a Canadian credential
  • Records proving your work experience: reference letters, pay records and contracts in your current occupation
  • Your Worker EOI, submitted through your Alberta.ca account

Your Employer's Side

  • A full time job offer or employment contract in your current occupation
  • A completed Employer Declaration and Authorization form
  • Business documents Alberta requests to confirm the employer and the job are genuine
  • A workplace on premises in Alberta, zoned for commercial or industrial operations
Questions

Alberta Opportunity Stream Questions, Answered

My PGWP is from a college in another province. Can I use this stream?

No. For the Alberta Opportunity Stream, a Post Graduation Work Permit only counts if it was issued to a graduate of an Alberta Advanced Education publicly funded post secondary institution, and your occupation must be related to your Alberta field of study. If your PGWP is from another province, we will assess you against the other AAIP streams and the Express Entry routes instead of spending your fees here.

My work permit expires while I am waiting. What happens?

This is the most dangerous timing issue in this stream. You must hold a valid work permit when you submit your application and when Alberta assesses it, and maintained status or restoration status does not count. If your permit is expiring, the renewal or extension strategy has to be planned before you enter the pool, not after you are invited. Tell us your expiry date in your first message and we will plan around it.

My employer will not complete their declaration. Can I still apply?

No. Alberta requires your employer's cooperation, including the Employer Declaration and Authorization form and business documents that confirm the job offer. Without them, the application cannot succeed. In our experience most employers cooperate once someone explains exactly what is being asked and why. We brief your employer directly, in plain language, so this does not become your problem to solve alone.

Is my EOI score the same as my CRS score?

No, and confusing them is expensive. Your Worker EOI score is Alberta's own points grid, out of 100, used only to rank you in Alberta's Worker Stream pool. Your CRS score belongs to the federal Express Entry system. The Alberta Opportunity Stream does not use CRS at all. If you also hold an Express Entry profile, that is a separate route with separate selection.

How much does it cost to enter the pool?

The Worker Expression of Interest fee is $135, and the application fee if you are invited is $1,500. Both are paid to the Government of Alberta and are non refundable. Our professional fees are confirmed with you in writing before any work starts.

Can you promise that I will be invited or nominated?

No, and no consultant honestly can. Invitations and nominations are decided by the Alberta Advantage Immigration Program alone. What we do is prepare your file so that your eligibility is real, your score reflects everything you are entitled to claim, and your documents prove it, which is the part of the outcome that preparation controls.

I work part time but more than 30 hours some weeks. Do I qualify?

No. Part time, casual and seasonal employees are ineligible for this stream regardless of hours worked. Your employment and your job offer must be genuinely full time.
